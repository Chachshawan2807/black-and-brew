## Overall Impression

The app has a consistent font foundation and readable body line-height, but small grid labels and mixed tracking rules weaken scanning in the busiest operational screens. The largest opportunity is to define a Thai-safe type scale and use it consistently across inventory, schedule, and shift status UI.

## What’s Working

- `Prompt`, `IBM Plex Sans Thai`, and `Inter` are declared as one shared stack and applied at the locale root.
- Body text uses `line-height: 1.6`; shared page title styles provide a common size hierarchy.
- Home task cards and shift cards group labels and values clearly. The time-based pastel colors help staff scan shift status.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Negative tracking is applied to every heading, including Thai.** Global `h1` to `h6` styles use `letter-spacing: -0.025em`, and shared page-title classes repeat it. This is a Latin headline convention applied to dense Thai glyph clusters and may crowd marks or reduce legibility. `docs/design.md` prioritizes Thai glyph integrity, while shared headings use a tighter line-height than body text. **Fix:** use neutral tracking for shared headings by default; apply tighter tracking only to specific Latin headings after visual review. Check Thai headings at narrow widths. Suggested follow-up: `$impeccable critique` after any approved typography change.

2. **[P1] Inventory card headers are 9px and low contrast.** Six operational labels use `text-[9px] text-foreground/45` and `truncate` in both desktop and mobile row layouts. These labels identify stock, order quantity, order point, target stock, unit, and source. They are hard to read on phones and may be confused while editing. **Fix:** raise operational labels to a legible compact size, then adjust wrapping or card layout to preserve the inline grid behavior. Suggested follow-up: `$impeccable critique` after any approved typography change.

3. **[P2] Time and status cues use 9px to 11px text.** Home shift cards use a 9px time pill, 10px range text, and 11px end-of-shift text. Schedule labels and presets also use 10px to 11px. These are secondary in hierarchy but operationally important. **Fix:** reserve the smallest sizes for tertiary metadata and use a consistent, readable minimum for shift and status cues. Suggested follow-up: `$impeccable critique` after any approved typography change.

4. **[P2] Wide tracking is shared with Thai labels.** `.bb-label-caps` uses `0.16em`; the schedule form label utility uses `0.12em`. Uppercase styling does not change Thai characters, while letter spacing still widens the labels and can make short Thai phrases look disconnected. **Fix:** make tracking opt-in for Latin-only labels or use neutral tracking on mixed-language labels. Suggested follow-up: `$impeccable critique` after any approved typography change.

5. **[P2] Staff names truncate in fixed-width shift cards.** Home employee names use 13px text with `truncate` in an 8.25rem card. The accessible name retains the full value, but sighted staff cannot see a longer name in the card. **Fix:** allow a two-line name or another visible way to reveal the full name without hiding the shift-time cue. Suggested follow-up: `$impeccable critique` after any approved typography change.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Busy shift manager scanning inventory:** faint 9px labels make it easier to enter a value in the wrong column.
- **Staff checking shifts on a phone:** truncated names and tiny time pills make it harder to confirm who is working and when.
- **Staff with reduced near vision:** recurring 9px to 11px text may require zoom or slower reading.

## Minor Observations

- The documented Thai body line-height is 1.6, while global headings and page titles use 1.5. Check that long Thai titles keep adequate space for upper and lower marks.
- The Home date title uses balanced wrapping, which is appropriate for headings. Long dynamic Thai titles still need a narrow-width check. The Web Interface Guidelines also recommend balanced or pretty wrapping for headings and explicit handling of long text: [Typography and content handling guidelines](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines/main/command.md).
- Schedule export rules that clamp holiday text to four lines and use 1.375 line-height are export-only. Do not treat them as ordinary screen typography.
